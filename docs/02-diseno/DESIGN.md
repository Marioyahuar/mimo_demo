## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the serene, nurturing ethos of an elevated boutique spa crafted exclusively for pets and their devoted guardians. The design direction merges **Organic Warmth** with **Modern Soft Minimalism**, evoking tranquility, tender care, hygiene, and boutique indulgence. 

The emotional objective is absolute reassurance: a pet parent should feel immediate calm, safety, and pampered delight upon entering the digital experience. We avoid clinical veterinary sterility or chaotic pet-store gamification. Instead, the interface breathes with generous spatial pads, gentle rounded volumes, tactile pill shapes, and a palette reminiscent of natural fleece, earthen pottery, and herbal steam baths.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from tactile organic materials and comforting spa rituals.

- **Primary (`#E8927C` - Soft Coral):** Dedicated to essential calls to action, active booking highlights, and pivotal focal moments. Radiates warmth, affection, and vitality without visual alarm.
- **Secondary (`#7BA68C` - Sage Green):** Used for herbal wellness indicators, positive status tags, secondary accents, and serene natural contrasts that ground the warm palette.
- **Tertiary / Container Tint (`#F5EFE6` - Soft Warm Alabaster):** Used for soft card surfaces, inset wells, table headers, and layered section backgrounds that gently contrast against the base canvas.
- **Neutral Dark (`#3B3530` - Warm Charcoal):** The bedrock for typography and structural icons. Far softer than harsh black, maintaining high readability with an earthen, tactile depth.
- **Base Canvas (`#FBF7F0` - Warm Cream Linen):** The default screen background, giving the entire viewport a peaceful, paper-like warmth.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Used selectively for raised interactive cards, dialogue sheets, and input fields to foster crisp elevation against the cream canvas.

## Typography

The typographical pairing balances boutique elegance with friendly, approachable clarity. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries romantic, soft editorial cadence. Its transitional curves feel luxurious, evoking high-end hospitality and mindful living. Letter spacing is slightly tightened at large scales to preserve visual cohesion.
- **Body, UI & Meta Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Features rounded geometric terminals, generous x-height, and wide counter spaces. It ensures fatigue-free readability during multi-step appointment scheduling, intake questionnaires, and treatment menus.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is rooted in generous, airy breathing room that keeps the digital interface unhurried and calming.

- **Grid Architecture:** A responsive 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (max width 1280px, centered) with 24px gutters. Reflows to an 8-column layout on tablet (16px gutters) and a 4-column system on mobile (16px gutters).
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Content sections must be delineated through whitespace rather than hard divider rules. Vertical section separation requires `space-3xl` (4.5rem) on mobile and `space-4xl` (6rem) on desktop.
- **Padded Sanctuaries:** Card elements and interactive containers must maintain a minimum internal padding of `space-lg` (1.5rem) to ensure treatment listings and pet profile previews never feel cramped.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids harsh, technical drop shadows in favor of **Warm Ambient Diffusion**. Shadows emulate warm morning sunlight falling over plush natural linen.

- **Elevation Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the main backdrop (`#FBF7F0`) and subtle container groups (`#F5EFE6`).
- **Elevation Level 1 (Resting Cards & Modules):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(59, 53, 48, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(59, 53, 48, 0.03)`. Surfaces sit seamlessly above the linen background using pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Elevation Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Popovers):** `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(59, 53, 48, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(59, 53, 48, 0.04)`. Elements physically lift with a smooth 200ms ease-out transition.
- **Coral Glow Elevation (Primary Action Hover):** For primary booking triggers: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -2px rgba(232, 146, 124, 0.35)`. Provides an inviting, tactile warmth rather than mechanical depth.
- **Outlines & Borders:** Soft, low-contrast border lines only when required for strict accessibility: `1px solid rgba(59, 53, 48, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects warmth, gentleness, and safety. There are no sharp corners; every visual element is softened to eliminate cold angles.

- **Pill Geometry (Full Rounded):** Applied systematically to buttons, chips, search bars, filter badges, and status pills (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Surface Geometry (Rounded-2xl / Rounded-3xl):** Standard service cards, pet photo frames, and intake cards feature generous curves between `1.5rem` (`24px`) and `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Organic Avatar Cutouts:** Pet profiles and decorative imagery utilize soft organic squircle masks or rounded pebble silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in Soft Coral (`#E8927C`), text in pure `#FFFFFF`, fully rounded pill (`rounded-full`). Padding: `14px 28px`. Font: `label-lg`. On hover, applies a `-2px` subtle Y-lift accompanied by the Coral Glow elevation.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface tint in `#F5EFE6` or pure `#FFFFFF` with a warm border `1.5px solid rgba(59, 53, 48, 0.12)`. Text in Warm Charcoal (`#3B3530`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, Warm Charcoal text, gentle hover background in `rgba(245, 239, 230, 0.7)`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Tag / Category Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height 36px, `padding: 0 16px`. Default state uses `#F5EFE6` surface with `#3B3530` text. Selected state transforms into Sage Green (`#7BA68C`) background with white text, or Soft Coral (`#E8927C`) for highlighted active selections.

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Text Inputs:** Pure white background, fully rounded or `rounded-2xl` corners, subtle warm border `1.5px solid rgba(59, 53, 48, 0.1)`. Focus state: border color transitions to `#E8927C` paired with a delicate halo: `0 0 0 3px rgba(232, 146, 124, 0.15)`. Floating labels utilize `Plus Jakarta Sans` medium.

### Cards (Spa Services & Pet Profiles)
- **Service Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, `border-radius: 28px`, internal padding `24px`. Soft ambient elevation level 1. Image containers within cards adopt a matching internal `border-radius: 20px`.
- **Pet Profile Badges:** Inset container in `#F5EFE6`, displaying circular pet avatars with micro-tags for coat length, breed, and temperament sensitivities.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes:** Circular rounded controls. Unchecked: `1.5px solid rgba(59, 53, 48, 0.25)` on `#FFFFFF`. Checked: Solid fill `#7BA68C` (Sage Green) containing a white checkmark or inner dot.

### Specialized Component: Treatment Add-On Selector
- A soft tactile card toggled via click. Features an inset micro-pill badge for aromatherapies, hydro-baths, and blueberry facials. When active, card shifts border to `#E8927C` with a background wash of `rgba(232, 146, 124, 0.05)`.
